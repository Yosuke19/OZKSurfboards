Asym

The ASYM is built for a new sensation — a board that feels different rail-to-rail by combining asymmetrical design with a versatile fin setup. Tuned for speed, flow, and control, the ASYM lets you dial in two distinct personalities in one board: more drive and hold on one side, and a looser, more playful feeling on the other. Depending on how you set the fins, you can enjoy a completely different ride while keeping the same fast, responsive outline.

Key features:

• Asymmetrical concept — engineered to deliver a different feel on each rail for a fresh, dynamic surfing sensation.
• 3-fin option (Quad + Twin) — quad on one side for drive and hold, twin on the other for a looser, faster release and stylish flow.
• 4-fin option (Quad + Quad) — maximum speed and projection with extra hold through turns when you want a more locked-in feel.
• Swallow tail option — adds bite, acceleration, and control while keeping the board lively and responsive.

The ASYM is all about experimentation and feel — change the fin setting and you unlock a new personality, letting you enjoy different sensations from the same board.
